## About This Game

### Tactical Grid Combat Meets Card Collection

**Summoner's Grid** is a tactical grid-based RPG card game where every card you collect shapes your strategy. Place summons on a 12×14 battlefield, command them through a rich turn-based combat system, and build your collection through pack openings that feel like unwrapping treasure.

### Deep Strategic Gameplay
- **Turn-Based Tactics** — Draw, Level, Action, End. Every phase matters.
- **72 Unique Cards** — Actions, Buildings, Quests, Counters, Reactions, and Advances
- **7 Species** — Gignen warriors, ethereal Fae, stalwart Stonehearts, feral Wilderlings, celestial Angar, cunning Demar, and calculating Creptilis
- **Role Advancement** — Evolve your summons from Tier 1 to powerful Tier 3 roles like Berserker, Warlock, or Battle Dancer
- **Effect Stack** — Counter your opponent's plays with speed-priority card interactions

### Collect & Build
- **Open Packs** — Reveal cards one by one with escalating excitement. Will you pull a Myth?
- **5 Rarities** — Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legend, and Myth. Rarer cards are genuinely stronger.
- **Build Decks** — Choose 3 summons from your collection and take them into battle
- **Species Power** — Each species excels in different stats. Find your playstyle.

### Play Your Way
- **Battle vs AI** — Test your deck against a strategic AI opponent
- **Random Deck** — Jump in with procedurally generated decks for variety
- **Watch AI vs AI** — Study strategies by spectating AI matches
- **Earn by Playing** — Win coins from every match to buy more packs

### Key Features
- Verified game engine with 107 automated tests — no formula bugs
- Procedural audio — dynamic battle music and 12 unique sound effects
- Dark fantasy aesthetic with AI-generated pixel art
- Full tutorial for new players
- Persistent card collection that grows over time
