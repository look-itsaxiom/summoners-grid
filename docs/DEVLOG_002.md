# Devlog #2: The AI Got Smarter and Now There Are Reasons to Come Back

Hey, it's Hex.

Three updates dropped today. All free, all in the same download. Here's what changed.

## AI Difficulty: Pick Your Punishment

You can now choose between Easy, Normal, and Hard in Settings.

**Easy** is for learning. The AI fumbles card plays 40% of the time and occasionally forgets to move its units. It's not throwing the game — it's just distracted. Good for your first few matches or for testing deck builds without pressure.

**Normal** is what shipped in v0.1.0. Five-priority card evaluation, smart HP-weighted targeting, heals-before-attacks logic. It's a solid opponent that makes you think.

**Hard** adds territory awareness. The AI now evaluates which of your summons are the biggest strategic threat — higher level units score higher, and anything in its home territory gets prioritized for removal. It doesn't cheat. It just cares more.

## Achievements: 10 Milestones, Real Rewards

There are now 10 achievements, each giving you coins when you unlock them for the first time:

- **First Blood** (100 coins) — Win your first battle
- **On Fire** (150 coins) — Win 3 in a row  
- **Unstoppable** (300 coins) — Win 5 in a row
- **Veteran** (200 coins) — Play 10 matches
- **Champion** (500 coins) — Win 10 matches
- **Pack Rat** (200 coins) — Open 5 packs
- **Collector** (300 coins) — Own 50 cards
- **Myth Hunter** (250 coins) — Pull a Mythic card
- **Deck Master** (200 coins) — Win with a custom deck
- **Moving Up** (150 coins) — Reach Apprentice rank

When you unlock one, a gold toast notification slides down from the top of the screen. It feels good. Trust me.

Check your progress in Settings — there's a full list showing what you've unlocked and what's left.

## Daily Challenges

Every day, you get a new challenge with a bonus coin reward (150-300 coins). The challenge rotates automatically — today might be "Win in 6 turns or fewer" and tomorrow might be "Win on Hard difficulty."

Seven challenge types: Speed Run, Flawless, Underdog, Card Master, Territory Control, Random Hero, and Triple Kill. The active challenge shows on the main menu so you always know what you're going for.

This stacks with the daily login bonus (100 coins) and regular match rewards. A good day of playing can net you 500+ coins easily — that's almost two packs.
